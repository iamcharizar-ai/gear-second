# The routine

A seven-day hypertrophy split built for one physique, not a generic PPL. The seeded routines in the app (`src/data/templates.ts`) are this document in code.

## What it is optimising for

**Goal:** muscle for looks: wide shoulders, wide and thick back, full upper chest, big arms, tight waist, respectable legs.

**Specialisation, not equal volume.**

| Tier | Muscles | Weekly working sets (direct / effective*) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — maximum | Side delts | 20 / 20 |
| | Upper chest | 12 / 12 |
| | Lats | 12 / 17 (+ pull-ups from the Arbor morning block) |
| | Mid / upper back | 10 / 18 |
| 2 — high | Rear delts | 9 / 12.5 |
| | Triceps | 12 / 15.5 |
| | Biceps | 11 / 17.5 |
| | Mid / lower chest (flys) | 6 / 12 |
| 3 — normal | Abs | 9 / 9 |
| | Quads | 7 / 7 |
| | Hamstrings | 5 / 5 |
| | Calves | 4 / 4 |
| | Inner thigh | 2 / 2 |
| none direct | Front delts (7.5 indirect from pressing), traps, forearms, obliques, lower back | — |

\* Effective = a set counts 1 for the exercise's primary muscle and ½ for each secondary one. This is how the app's heatmap counts too, so you can check reality against this table in **Stats**.

## How to run it

- **Every set is a working set.** Take the last set of each exercise to failure; earlier sets stop with 0-1 reps left. No warm-up sets are logged: warm up, then log what counts.
- **Progression is double progression**, and the app does it for you: stay in the rep range; when every set hits the top of the range, the app suggests +2.5 kg and drops you to the bottom of the range. Otherwise, beat last time's reps.
- **Supersets** pair things that do not compete, on equipment that is next to each other, to cut time without cutting stimulus.
- **Sessions are short** (16-18 sets, about 45-55 minutes) because you train every day; each muscle gets 48 hours or more before its next hard session.
- If a machine is taken, swap for the alternative in the exercise's note. The priority is the target muscle, not the tool.

## The week

### Mon · Upper Chest + Side Delts — 17 sets
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Target | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incline Dumbbell Press (30°) | 4 × 6-10 | Upper chest | The heavy upper-chest movement. Dumbbells let the elbows travel and the pec stretch; low incline keeps the front delt from taking over. |
| Low-to-High Cable Fly | 3 × 10-15 | Upper chest | Loads the clavicular fibres along their line of pull with constant tension, no triceps limit. |
| Pec Deck ⇄ | 3 × 10-15 | Mid / lower chest | Stable, deep stretch, easy to take to failure safely. Your fly-based chest work instead of flat bench. |
| Dumbbell Lateral Raise ⇄ | 4 × 12-20 | Side delts | Superset with the pec deck (grab the dumbbells, stand by the machine). |
| Cable Crunch | 3 × 10-15 | Abs | Loadable spinal flexion; no oblique work. |

### Tue · Lats + Rear Delts + Biceps — 16 sets
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Target | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull-Up | 4 × 6-12 | Lats | Your favourite lift and a great lat builder. At 4 × 12, move to Weighted Pull-Up. |
| Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown | 3 × 8-12 | Lats | Shoulder-friendly lat line, stretch at the top, no bodyweight limit so you can keep going after pull-ups. |
| Chest-Supported Row | 3 × 8-12 | Mid / upper back | Elbows out for rhomboids and mid traps. Supported, so the lower back is not the limit. |
| Reverse Pec Deck | 3 × 12-20 | Rear delts | Fixed path keeps it on the rear delt instead of turning into a row. |
| Incline Dumbbell Curl | 3 × 8-12 | Biceps (long head) | Stretched position, strong tension where it counts. |

### Wed · Legs — 18 sets
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Target | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leg Press | 4 × 8-12 | Quads, glutes | Heavy, deep, stable. Beginner legs grow fast on this without squat technique work. |
| Romanian Deadlift | 2 × 8-12 | Hamstrings, glutes | The one hinge: loaded stretch for the hamstrings. Two sets keeps lower-back fatigue small. |
| Leg Extension ⇄ | 3 × 10-15 | Quads | Rectus femoris, which the press does not train well. |
| Seated Leg Curl ⇄ | 3 × 10-15 | Hamstrings | Curls in a lengthened position. Superset with extensions. |
| Hip Adduction | 2 × 12-15 | Inner thigh | Fills the gap the press leaves. |
| Standing Calf Raise | 4 × 10-15 | Calves | Paused in the stretch. |

### Thu · Side Delts + Triceps — 16 sets
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Target | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cable Lateral Raise (behind the body) | 4 × 10-15 | Side delts | Tension in the stretched position, where dumbbells have none. Your top-priority lift of the week. |
| Machine Lateral Raise | 3 × 12-20 | Side delts | Second resistance profile; easy to add partials after failure. |
| Cable Rear Delt Fly ⇄ | 3 × 12-20 | Rear delts | Cross-cable version has a long range. |
| Triceps Pushdown ⇄ | 3 × 10-15 | Triceps | You respond to these. Superset with the rear-delt fly on the same cable column. |
| Overhead Cable Extension | 3 × 10-15 | Triceps (long head) | Long head under stretch: the part that makes the arm look thick from the side and back. |

### Fri · Back Thickness + Biceps — 17 sets
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Target | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chest-Supported Row | 4 × 8-12 | Mid / upper back | The main thickness lift, done first. |
| Seated Cable Row (wide grip) | 3 × 10-12 | Mid / upper back | Elbows high, big scapular range. |
| Lat Pulldown | 3 × 10-15 | Lats | Second lat exposure of the week. |
| Straight-Arm Pulldown | 2 × 12-15 | Lats | Lats without any biceps, so it does not eat into arm recovery. |
| Preacher Curl | 3 × 8-12 | Biceps (short head) | Stable, hard at the bottom. |
| Bayesian Cable Curl | 2 × 10-15 | Biceps (long head) | Stretch under cable tension. |

### Sat · Chest Flys + Side Delts — 18 sets

|---|---|---|---|
| Incline Machine Press | 3 × 8-12 | Upper chest | Second upper-chest press; the machine removes stabilising so the pec is the limit. |

## Calisthenics around it

The Arbor coach plans your skill work around this split:

- The **morning block** (at home) skips whatever the gym trains hard that evening: no push skills on Mon / Thu / Sat, no pull skills on Tue / Fri, no leg skills on Wed.
- **Gym add-ons** are skills that need the gym (dips, German hang, bar swings, monkey bars) and are placed on the day that already trains those muscles. They show at the top of the workout as **Skill work**. Do them first, while fresh, and keep them short.
- Strength skills (push / pull / legs) get at least one day off between sessions.

## What to change when

- **Arms growing faster than everything else?** Drop Sunday's curl and extension superset first.
- **Shoulders or elbows complaining?** Swap dumbbell laterals for the machine and cap pushdown volume.
- **Legs lagging badly later on?** Add a second, short leg session (leg press + leg curl) on Sunday instead of the arm pump.
- Recheck volumes in **Stats → Muscles** after two or three weeks; adjust one exercise at a time.
